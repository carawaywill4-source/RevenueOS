# TributeReady organic and future paid-search campaign pack

This file is a launch brief, not authorization to spend. Paid campaigns require
an advertiser account, billing approval, conversion validation, and a budget.

## Positioning

**Promise:** Tell their story once. Review every word. Receive a coordinated,
print-ready memorial collection.

**Proof points:**

- Family-supplied facts only; no invented relatives, beliefs, dates, or events.
- Editable preview before purchase.
- Four-panel program, memorial card, thank-you card, and private memorial page.
- One-time $34.99 payment with no subscription.
- Private by default with 30-day source-file deletion.

Do not lead with “AI.” Do not claim permanence, guaranteed emotional outcomes,
professional funeral services, or human authorship.

## Organic landing-page mapping

- `/funeral-program-maker`: program, order-of-service, and printing searches.
- `/obituary-writer`: obituary drafting and factual-accuracy searches.
- `/celebration-of-life-program`: secular and personality-led program searches.
- `/resources/*`: informational queries; each guide should point to the closest
  free-preview entry.

## Future Google Search campaign

Use keyword-targeted Search only. Bereavement products are a sensitive-interest
category: never use remarketing lists, Customer Match, lookalikes, custom
segments built from grief or bereavement behavior, or language implying that
Google disclosed a person’s loss.

## Negative keywords

jobs, salary, career, software for funeral homes, funeral home management,
cremation near me, funeral home near me, casket, embalming, burial plot, death
certificate, free download, free Word template, free Canva template, Photoshop,
pirated, torrent, wholesale printing, flowers, insurance, livestream.

Review search-term reports before adding broader negatives. Do not exclude
“free preview,” because the product genuinely offers one.

## Initial measurement gates

Do not launch paid search until first-party events reliably report:

1. Landing session
2. Builder start
3. Draft generated
4. Checkout created
5. Paid order
6. Fulfilled order

Start with exact and phrase match. A campaign should not scale until it has at
least 20 attributable paid orders and a stable fulfilled-order conversion
rate. Set a target customer-acquisition cost only after actual gross margin,
refunds, and support costs have been measured.
