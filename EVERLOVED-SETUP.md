# Ever Loved vendor listing — setup pack

Everything below is ready to paste. Sources: Ever Loved help articles
[280](https://support.everloved.com/article/280-how-to-sell-your-services-on-ever-loved)
and [196](https://support.everloved.com/article/196-how-to-create-a-marketplace-account).

Why this channel: it is the only free, self-serve marketplace with real US
bereaved traffic. No listing fee, no subscription. Verification is usually
under 48 hours. They take 10% of (listing price + shipping), and the first
payout starts 10 business days after a sale.

---

## Step 1 — Sign up (5 minutes)

Go to the Ever Loved vendor sign-up page.

- **Tick the box** "I work in the death care industry, at a charity or at an
  event space." Without this the correct options do not appear.
- Choose **"I am selling something else"** — we sell digital files, not a
  physical product they ship.
- **Use `care@tributeready.org`, not a personal Gmail.** Their help page says
  signing up with a personal address and no website delays verification. We
  have both a business domain and a live site, so we should clear quickly.
- **Website:** `https://tributeready.org`

**What we're selling** (paste into the "tell us what you're selling" field):

> Print-ready memorial file collections for families arranging a funeral or
> celebration of life. The family answers a few guided questions about the
> person and receives a print-ready funeral program, a memorial keepsake card,
> a matching thank-you card, and a private online memorial page. Everything is
> delivered as high-resolution PDFs they can print anywhere. We also publish a
> free library of obituary templates, order-of-service outlines for seven
> traditions, eulogy examples, and verified public-domain funeral readings.

---

## Step 2 — Wait for verification

Usually under 48 hours. If nothing by then, email `support@everloved.com`.
Nothing else can be done until this clears, which is why signing up early
matters more than getting the listing perfect.

---

## Step 3 — Payment details

Stripe Express account, set up from User Settings → Payment details.

**Connect a checking account. Stripe rejects savings accounts for direct
deposit.** This is the most common thing to get wrong.

## Step 4 — The listing

### Title

> Complete Memorial Collection — Funeral Program, Keepsake Card, Thank-You
> Card, and Private Memorial Page

### Price

**List at $38.99.**

Ever Loved deducts 10% of the listing price, so $38.99 nets $35.09 — which
holds our $34.99 site price roughly level. Listing at $34.99 would net $31.49
and quietly make every marketplace sale worth less than a direct one.

There is no shipping. The files are delivered by email.

> **Written from your memories, not from a template.**

> **What you receive**

## One thing you should know before you list

Ever Loved shows the refund policy on every product page, next to competitors
who mostly offer money-back guarantees. You chose to keep our current wording,
which is a legitimate call for a customized digital product — I'm flagging it
only because on a marketplace it sits directly beside the alternatives, where
it's more visible than it is on our own site. Easy to revisit later if the
listing gets views but no sales.
